Privacy Policy

Pix Piano

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Holy App ("we") sets out below how the iOS app "Pix Piano" (the "App") handles information. This is an English translation of the Japanese privacy policy; if there is any difference, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Accounts and on-device information

The App does not offer account registration that asks for your name, address, phone number or email address. It stores settings such as tone, keys and visual effects, whether the ad and tracking explanation has been shown, and the expiry of features temporarily unlocked through ads on your device. Performances are not saved and nothing is synced to the cloud.

2. Usage analytics

The App uses Firebase Analytics to measure events such as starting and summarizing a performance, changing settings, and unlocking features through ads. Events may include the tone, visual effect, number of keys, number of performances, playing time, settings and unlock route. Individual pitches, per-key history and user IDs issued by us are not sent.

Analytics data is sent only when analytics storage is allowed in Google's consent management settings, or when consent is determined not to be required in your region. If you decline or the status is unknown, analytics collection stays off.

3. Advertising and consent management

The App shows ads through Google AdMob and manages consent with the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). For ad delivery, ad measurement and fraud prevention, Google and its advertising partners may process the advertising identifier, device and app information, and information about ad impressions and interactions. The items collected and how long they are kept follow each service's settings and policies.

After UMP consent is checked, the App shows an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) explanation and Apple's permission prompt when needed. Your ATT choice applies to the use of the advertising identifier.

4. Crash diagnostics

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose problems. When a crash occurs, crash details, stack traces, the app version, device and OS information, and diagnostic identifiers may be sent to Google. This information is used to improve the App's stability.

6. Deletion and contact

Settings stored on your device are deleted when you delete the App from your device. Retention periods and deletion methods for information processed by Google and other services follow each provider's policies. For questions about this policy or how information is handled, please contact us through the Holy App support page.

7. Changes

If the App's features or applicable laws change, we will revise this page and state the date of the update.

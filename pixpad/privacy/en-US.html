Privacy Policy

Pix Pad

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Holy App ("we") explains how user information is handled in the iOS app "Pix Pad" (the "app").

1. Accounts and data stored in the app

The app has no account registration or sign-in. It stores items such as the expiry time of pads unlocked through ads and whether the initial ad explanation has been shown on your device. The app has no function that saves this information to our own server.

2. Usage analytics

The app uses Firebase Analytics. When you choose to allow analytics storage, it records events such as home screen views, pad selection, playing time, number of interactions, and ad loading and display results. If consent cannot be confirmed, the app stops recording Firebase Analytics events. Free-form text, audio content, and touch positions are not included in analytics events.

3. Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to show banner ads and rewarded ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK and advertising partners may process device identifiers, approximate location estimated from IP addresses, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic and performance information to deliver ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and diagnose services.

Depending on your region, the app shows the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent screen. After ad settings, it shows an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) explanation and Apple's permission prompt when required. You can use the app even if you do not allow ATT.

4. Crash and diagnostic information

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash and diagnostic information. This information is used to identify and fix problems.

5. Third-party services

For the advertising, analytics, and crash diagnostics described above, the app uses services provided by Google LLC, including Firebase, Google Mobile Ads, and UMP. The information each service handles and how long it is kept follow each provider's policies.

6. Sharing and retention

Companies that provide advertising, analytics, and diagnostic services process information to the extent necessary to provide their services. We do not collect names, email addresses, or similar information through the app, and we do not sell such information. Retention periods and deletion methods for information collected by third-party services follow each provider's terms.

7. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy in response to changes in laws, app features, or the services we use. When we do, the latest version and update date will be posted on this page.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy or how information is handled in the app, please contact us below.

Privacy Policy

Suji Board

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Holy App ("we") describes below how information is handled in Suji Board (the "App").

1. Numbers, calculations and settings

Numbers and calculations you enter are processed on your device. The App does not send them to our servers or to any speech synthesis server. There is no feature that saves calculation history to an account or syncs it with iCloud.

Settings such as the voice, digit grouping and reading style are stored in the App. Deleting the App also deletes these settings. The App does not use account registration, microphone recording, or location services such as GPS. The advertising SDK may estimate your approximate location from your IP address.

2. Voices

The App plays iOS voices or recorded voices bundled with the App on your device. VOICEVOX voices are generated in advance, and the App does not send numbers to an external voice generation service while you use it.

3. Crash diagnostics

To find and fix problems, the App uses Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google LLC. When the App crashes, the stack trace, device, OS and App information, and other diagnostics are sent to Google. Because Firebase Analytics is also used, standard events just before a crash (such as app launches and sessions) may be included in diagnostic records. Numbers, calculations and spoken content you enter are not recorded.

Firebase privacy information / Google Privacy Policy

4. Usage statistics

To see the total number of users and active users, the App uses Firebase Analytics, provided by Google LLC. Firebase Analytics processes standard events such as app launches and sessions, an identifier assigned to each installation, and App, device and OS information. It may also estimate a rough region from your IP address. Firebase Analytics does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA) or IDFV, and automatic screen reporting is disabled. We may collect usage statistics to improve the App. The App does not send privacy-related information such as user IDs, numbers, calculations or spoken content you enter, or text you write. Where consent is required, such as in Europe, an identifier is stored on the device for these figures only if you consent through Google User Messaging Platform.

Firebase privacy information / Data collection in Firebase Analytics

5. Sending feedback

Only when you choose to send feedback (for example from Share Your Thoughts in Settings), the App stores the text you entered, along with your device model, iOS version, App version and language setting, in Cloud Firestore (Firebase), a service provided by Google LLC. To prevent abuse, Firebase App Check verifies that the submission comes from the App. We use feedback only to improve the App and respond to it, and we do not link it to information that identifies you. Please do not include any personally identifiable information in your feedback.

6. Other apps

Settings introduces our other apps. Selecting one opens Apple's App Store page. Apple's policies apply to information handled on the App Store.

7. Advertising and consent

The App shows a Google AdMob banner ad at the bottom of the calculator screen. No ads are shown in Settings. There are no rewarded ads or purchases.

To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google and its advertising partners may process your IP address, approximate location, device identifiers, ad impression and interaction data, and App interaction, performance and diagnostic data. Numbers, calculations and spoken content you enter are not passed to the advertising SDK.

Where required, the App asks for consent to ads and Analytics through Google User Messaging Platform, and you can change or withdraw it from Privacy under Support in Settings. You can choose whether to allow tracking on Apple's screen. You can use the App without allowing it. If you do not allow tracking, the App does not track you using the advertising identifier (IDFA).

How Google uses information in advertising

8. Sharing and retention

We do not sell your information. We keep feedback for as long as needed to consider improvements and delete it when no longer needed. Information held by third-party diagnostic and analytics services is retained and deleted according to each provider's policies. Except where disclosure is required by law, we do not provide information to third parties for purposes other than those described here.

9. Changes and contact

When we change features or how information is handled, we will update this page. Please contact us through the Holy App Facebook page.

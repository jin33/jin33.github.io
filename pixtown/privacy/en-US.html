Privacy Policy

Pix Town — Last updated: October 24, 2026

Holy App explains how information is handled in Pix Town.

1. App data

The app requires no account, photos, or recordings. It stores local preferences such as whether the tracking explanation has been shown, whether the initial play hint has appeared, and which interaction hints have been completed that day. It does not send a history of playground actions to our own server.

2. Advertising and consent

The app displays Google AdMob banner ads. Google and advertising partners may process IP addresses, approximate location, device identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and improve services. Google's UMP manages consent where required. Apple's App Tracking Transparency choice is reflected in advertising. Denying tracking does not prevent use of the app.

Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information

3. Diagnostics

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify and improve stability issues. Crash stack traces, app version, device and operating system information, and a diagnostic installation identifier are sent to Google. We do not add account names or playground content to crash reports. Firebase Analytics is not included.

4. Third parties

We use third-party services for advertising, consent management, and crash reporting. We do not otherwise sell or share information we obtain, except when required by law.

5. Retention and deletion

Local preferences are removed when the app is deleted. Information handled by advertising services is subject to their retention and deletion policies.

6. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app features or legal requirements change. The latest version and update date will appear here.

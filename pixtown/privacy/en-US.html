Privacy Policy

Pix Town — Last updated: November 12, 2026

Holy App explains how information is handled in Pix Town.

1. App data

The app requires no account, photos, or recordings. It stores local preferences such as whether the tracking explanation has been shown, whether the initial play hint has appeared, and which interaction hints have been completed that day. It does not send a history of playground actions to our own server.

2. Advertising and consent

The app displays Google AdMob banner ads. Google and advertising partners may process IP addresses, approximate location, device identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and improve services. Google's UMP manages consent where required. Apple's App Tracking Transparency choice is reflected in advertising. Denying tracking does not prevent use of the app.

Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information

3. Usage analysis and diagnostics

To improve the App, we use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google LLC, to collect usage statistics and crash information. The App does not send privacy-related information such as user IDs. Where consent is required, such as in Europe, an identifier is stored on the device for these statistics only if you consent. You can change or withdraw your consent from Privacy in the menu.

Feedback you send from Share Your Thoughts in the menu is stored, together with your device model, iOS version, App version and language setting, in Cloud Firestore provided by Google LLC. We use it only to improve the App.

4. Third parties

We use third-party services for advertising, consent management, usage analysis, crash reporting, and feedback storage. We do not otherwise sell or share information we obtain, except when required by law.

5. Retention and deletion

Local preferences are removed when the app is deleted. Information handled by advertising and analytics services is subject to their retention and deletion policies. Feedback is retained for as long as needed to consider improvements and deleted when no longer needed.

6. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app features or legal requirements change. The latest version and update date will appear here.
